## Evidence status

Document-only checks can establish reference/ID completeness, allowed token contrast, SVG structure and official format compliance. They cannot establish runtime authorization, deletion, content correctness, accessibility or legal compliance. Keep AC-01–19 and the runtime portion of AC-20 NOT RUN until an implementation is tested. DQ-1 blocks final brand adoption only; OD-001/OD-002 block real-family operation only.
